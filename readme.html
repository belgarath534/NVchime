NVChime 2.0

Customize your NVDA startup and exit sounds with powerful new features including schedule mode, random mode, and community sound pack support.

New in 2.0

  • Random mode: plays a different sound every time NVDA starts
  • Schedule mode: different sounds based on time of day
  • Day of week overrides: special sounds for specific days
  • Custom sound label: give your custom WAV a friendly name
  • Community sound pack import (.nvchime-pack files)

Sound Modes

  • Pack sound: choose from the built-in sound pack
  • Custom WAV: use any WAV file from your computer
  • Random: plays a random sound from all available sounds
  • Schedule: different sounds for morning, afternoon, evening, and night
  • Disabled: no sound

Recommended Setup

Go to NVDA Settings, General, and uncheck "Play sound when starting or exiting NVDA" so only your NVChime sound plays.

How to Use

  1. Open NVDA menu with NVDA+N
  2. Go to Preferences, then Settings
  3. Find the NVChime category
  4. Configure your sounds and hit OK
